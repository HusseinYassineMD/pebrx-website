Discovering a Target

Mission

PebRx is developing novel therapeutics and diagnostics to detect and treat APOE4-driven brain inflammation — addressing Alzheimer's disease at its molecular root, before irreversible damage occurs. Our lead program, the BRI-Series of brain-penetrant cPLA2 inhibitors, targets the hyperactivated lipid inflammation pathway that up to 25% of the U.S. population — APOE4 carriers — is uniquely at risk from. Paired with companion PET imaging to identify and monitor patients, BRI-Series is the first step in a pipeline built to bring precision, disease-modifying therapies to the largest untreated genetic risk group in Alzheimer's disease.

Platform Expansion

Beyond Alzheimer's disease, PebRx sees future potential for cPLA2-targeted therapies in additional neurovascular and inflammatory conditions where blood-brain barrier disruption and lipid-mediated inflammation play a pathological role:

  • Traumatic brain injury (TBI)
  • Stroke
  • Acute neuroinflammatory disorders
  • CAA in Down Syndrome
  • Sporadic CAA
